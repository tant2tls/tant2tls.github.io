I am an AI Research Resident at Qualcomm AI Research, working on 2D image, 3D content, and video generation — with an emphasis on distillation and quantization that make modern generative models deployable on edge devices. I graduated early from HUST in 3.5 years with a B.Sc. in Computer Science (GPA 3.69 / 4.0), finishing among the Top 3 graduating students and delivering the class graduation speech.

My research sits at the boundary between model design and systems: I want to understand AI end-to-end, from the mathematics of learning dynamics to the GPU architectures that make these models run at scale. I am currently applying to PhD programs to pursue this direction.
